      Leave the game?

      You'll lose your spot in the snake. Your handle and photo stay filled in, so jumping back in is quick.

      How it works

      1. Join the tail. Add your handle and photo (optional). You're one segment of a giant snake of real people.
      2. Tap to lead. During each 30-second turn, tap anywhere. Most taps when the timer ends becomes the next head.
      3. Get seen. While you lead, everyone in the snake sees your profile and can open it with one tap.

      FAQ

      What is plsfollow.me?

      plsfollow.me is a free multiplayer snake game you play in your browser. Every player is one segment of a giant snake made of strangers. Each head leads for 30 seconds, and while you lead, everyone in the snake sees your Instagram, TikTok, X, Threads, YouTube or Twitch profile.

      How do I become the head of the snake?

      Tap anywhere on the screen during the current leader's 30-second turn. Whoever has the most taps when the timer runs out leads next. If nobody taps, the next person in line leads.

      Can plsfollow.me get me more followers?

      It gives your profile a spotlight: while you lead, everyone in your snake sees your handle and can open your profile with one tap. Following is always their choice, so there are no follow-for-follow rules and no guaranteed numbers. Today's most-opened profiles appear on the Spotlight board.

      Is plsfollow.me free?

      Yes. The game is free with no account and no download. The only paid option is "Become a bot": for $5, a bot plays as your profile for 30 days and is marked as promoted.

      Does it work on phones?

      Yes. It runs in any modern mobile or desktop browser. Steer with your finger or mouse, or with the arrow keys and A/D on a keyboard.

      What are the purple trolls?

      Goblin-like trolls roam the arena. Bump one with your head and you lose 5 seconds of your turn; let one hit your body and your snake breaks in two. A head with no tail that touches a troll is knocked out.

      How do I steal people from another snake?

      Steer your head into another snake's body. Everyone from that point to the tail joins your snake. Grab a lightning boost to move faster while you hunt.

      What happens to my photo and handle?

      Your handle, platform and photo are remembered in your own browser so you don't have to re-enter them. Photos you play with are kept on the server only while you're playing.
